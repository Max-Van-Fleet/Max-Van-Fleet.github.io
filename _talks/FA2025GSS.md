# Abstract

Bandit problems are foundational to reinforcement learning as they provide a simple environment for analyzing key questions in Markov Decision Processes and modern RL. We re-work the analysis of Langevin Monte Carlo - Thompson Sampling under the new framework of When and Why randomized exploration works to provide minimax optimal bounds for linear contextual bandits, and our next goal is to extend the analysis to the general function approximation case with bounded eluder dimension. Next, I will discuss the deconvolution of spatial transcriptomics and cell clustering. All current workflows employ a two-step approach, beginning with clustering the scRNA data and then utilizing these clusters to perform deconvolution of spatial transcriptomics data. However, the clustering step is blind to the spatial transcriptomics data, leaving free information on the table. Therefore, we propose an optimization approach that simultaneously deconvolves spatial transcriptomics data and clusters the single-cell data. With the remaining time, I will discuss some of my very preliminary work on adding classical image processing techniques to modern diffusion/transformer models for image super-resolution.
