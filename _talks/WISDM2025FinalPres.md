This final presentation summarized our team’s work on data-model hybrid methods for single-image super-resolution at the 2025 WiSDM workshop. We investigated deep image priors and SR3-style diffusion models augmented with spatial regularization through modified activation functions and total-variation penalties, and we compared their performance across synthetic and CIFAR-10 examples. The presentation concluded with plans to develop adaptive regularized diffusion models and integrate them more closely with deep image prior architectures.
