This midweek presentation provided an update on our team’s single-image super-resolution project at the 2025 WiSDM workshop. We discussed data-model hybrid approaches that combine deep image priors and diffusion models with spatial regularization, presented preliminary comparisons across several model variants, and outlined directions for integrating regularized deep image priors with SR3-style diffusion methods.
